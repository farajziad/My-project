# Lunar Autonomous labo Robot
I already just have started working on this project , this project is a robot that can work on space on Labo's , He can bring the items that need analyze to the Labo by a camera and an IA system , this Robot can avoid obstacles from he's way until he find the thing that he's looking for .

# The versions of this Robot
 This robot has 3 versions:
 # V1 : 
 The Robot has the only avoid obstacles option , so he can avoid obstacles without any probleme or getting stuck .
 # V2 :
 The Robot will be updated by An AI ultrasonics and the servo for avoiding obstacles on the 3 directions (Forward , Left , Right).
 # V3 : 
 The proototype is about to get completed the robot has a camera + an IA systeme that can read items and know if its necessary or not + a arm that can take items and put it in the Robot sack.

# The materiel used : 
 # V1 : 
  2 DC motors , L298N , arduino UNO R4 WI-FI , HC-SR04 the distance sensor . 
 # V2 : 
 2 DC motors , L298N , Arduino UNO R4 WI-FI , HC-SR04 , the Servo motor for HC-SR04 moving .
 # V3 : 
 2 DC motors , L298N , HC-SR04 , Servo motor , Camera , A WI-FI Micro controller (ESP32 or ESP 8266).
